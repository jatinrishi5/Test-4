Tax calendar

Key CRA dates for the 2026 tax year.

Next up: Fourth quarterly instalment on Dec 15, 2026, in 24 days.

  1. Dec 15, 2026

    Fourth quarterly instalment for individuals who pay by instalments

  2. Mar 1, 2027

    RRSP contribution deadline. T4, T4A and T5 slips due

  3. Mar 31, 2027

    T3 trust returns for trusts with a December 31 year end

  4. Apr 30, 2027

    Personal returns due, and any balance owing must be paid

  5. Jun 15, 2027

    Filing deadline for self-employed individuals and spouses

  6. Jun 30, 2027

    T2 return due for corporations with a December 31 year end

General information only. If a date falls on a weekend or holiday, the CRA treats a return as on time if filed the next business day. Confirm dates on canada.ca.
